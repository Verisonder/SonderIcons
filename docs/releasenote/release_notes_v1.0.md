# SonderIcons 1.0

Every app on a HyperOS phone in one look, without root.

## What it does

- Draws an icon for every app your look doesn't cover, from the app's own monochrome
  glyph where it has one, its shape, or its logo cut out of its icon.
- Writes them into the Theme backup theme. Apply Theme backup in Themes to see them.

## Looks

- Dark Nothing, a designed set shipped in the app.
- My theme: your applied theme's own icons, with the gaps filled in its colours.
- Light circles, dark and light squircles, and a custom shape of your own.
- Any installed icon pack, such as one made in Icon Pack Studio.
- Any designed look can be moved onto another shape, keeping its designs.

## Per app

- Auto, an image from your gallery or an icon pack, the first letter of its name, or the
  look's own icon. Size, stroke, cut-out sensitivity and crisp edges, with a home screen
  preview.
- Change a whole group at once: select apps by filter or search.

## Also

- Quick toggles drawn in the look's colours, or your own.
- Extras: second icons and aliases, pinned shortcuts, and how unknown icons are drawn.
- OLED, Dark and Light app themes.
- Restore the theme's own icons at any time.

## Needs

- Shizuku, and a Theme backup (made by Themes, Customize theme).
