# SonderIcons 1.1

## New

- **Alternative icons.** Apps that let you switch their icon (Ente, Morphe) are listed in
  Extras with every icon they ship, and all of them are drawn, so switching keeps the look.
- **Pick an icon.** A missing app's sheet offers, in one tap, the icon every installed
  pack has for that very app, or its first letter. Browsing a pack opens on the app's name.
- **Backgrounds for pictures.** A pack icon or picture used as it is can sit on nothing,
  the look's own background, or its shape in any colour.

## Fixed

- Apps whose only icon is an alias are themed under the name HyperOS looks up first.
