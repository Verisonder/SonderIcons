# SonderIcons 1.2.1

- **Pinned shortcuts get the full sheet.** Size, stroke and tuning, pictures from a pack
  or the gallery, backgrounds, and the home screen preview, as for any app; then Pin
  themed copy.
- **Drawn or as it is.** A chosen picture can be drawn as a glyph in your style or used
  whole, picked plainly in the sheet.
- **Unknown icons default to As they are.** Every app already has an icon from
  SonderIcons, so this only reaches shortcuts, and keeps themed copies untouched.

Installs over 1.2.
